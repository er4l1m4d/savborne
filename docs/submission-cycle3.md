## Description (250-word limit)

Savborne is a savings RPG built for Nimiq Pay. It turns saving NIM into a daily
habit: keep your NIM in a vault only you control, check in each day, and watch
your real balance become XP, streaks, items, missions, badges, and rank.

The vault is genuinely non-custodial. Onboarding generates a 24-word phrase on
your device and derives a vault address from it. Deposits are signed by your
Nimiq Pay wallet; withdrawals are signed in-browser by that phrase. Savborne
reads the vault on chain but cannot move a single luna, and losing the phrase
and your device means the NIM is unrecoverable. The app says so before you
start.

Every check-in is a real on-chain reading, never a self-reported number. Your
balance, growth, and consistency drive missions and brackets (Entry, Bronze,
Silver, Gold), and a streak that breaks when you skip a day. Players can duel
the AI, challenge friends, or climb the ranked leaderboard, which shows display
names only.

It is built for Nimiq users who want to save but keep forgetting, and for
anyone who wants a reason to open their wallet tomorrow. The loop is simple:
deposit NIM, check in daily, level up, and save for real.

Savborne uses Nimiq Pay at its core: wallet accounts for identity, signed
transactions for deposits, message signing for check-in and account deletion,
and Nimiq RPC for balance reads.

## Demo video outline (2-3 minutes)

1. Open Savborne inside Nimiq Pay and show the welcome screen.
2. Onboard: pick a character, generate the vault, back up the phrase, confirm.
3. Deposit NIM from the Nimiq Pay wallet; show the confirmed vault balance.
4. Check in; show the XP, streak, and mission awards from the real balance.
5. Open Missions, then Ranks; show the ranked lock and the check-in gate.
6. Play one AI battle: move grid, item use, result screen.
7. Withdraw, sign with the phrase, show the on-chain receipt.
8. Close on the home screen and the live URL.
