Onboarding UI - current review

The focused onboarding flow at 360, 390 and 430: five steps, shorter copy, closed disclosures, the Hero/Heroine picker and the Settings reference. Corrections go in NOTES.md, under the screen they belong to and naming the width where possible.

Before - the old /setup wizard

/setup - one page, five labelled dots, square panels, mint CTA

1. Welcome

/welcome - Step 1 of 5; brand, character art, headline, coral CTA, footer.

2. Create your vault

/connect - Step 2 of 5; short intro, can/cannot cards and the closed vault disclosure.

3. Create your vault - vault disclosure open

/connect - The closed How your vault works disclosure opened.

4. Create your vault - account picker

/connect - Step 2 of 5; approved wallet account.

5. Backup - 24 words

/connect - Step 3 of 5; the two-sentence risk, the word grid and Continue.

6. Backup - quiz

/connect - Step 3 of 5; four words from the written copy.

7. Backup - lock

/connect - Step 3 of 5; two-sentence passphrase note.

8. Backup - ready

/connect - Step 3 of 5; returning from Privacy shows the locked state.

9. Privacy notice

/privacy - Step 4 of 5; short version, two cards and the closed disclosure.

10. Privacy - public and storage open

/privacy - The closed What is public and where it is stored disclosure opened.

11. Name your character - Hero

/name - Step 5 of 5; Hero selected by default, preview updates live.

12. Name your character - Heroine

/name - The Heroine selection; the preview and picker update together.

13. Name - 16 characters

/name - Counter at 16/16; the CTA is enabled.

14. Name - error

/name - A server rejection renders the coral banner below the field.

15. Settings - Character

/settings - The same picker in Account; a change is saved with PATCH /api/players/me.

16. How Savborne works

/settings/how-it-works - The long-form reference linked from Settings > Help.

17. Balance error

/balance-error - Only for 503 rpc_unreachable; Try again leaves a one-shot flag for Home.

18. Gate - Preparing

/welcome?gate=preparing - Brand mark and one line; no spinner, no action.

19. Gate - Open inside Nimiq Pay

/welcome?gate=outside - Phone panel and explanation; no CTA.

20. Gate - Update Nimiq Pay

/welcome?gate=update - Update arrow, site link and the retry that re-runs the check.

Renders are 2x device-scale PNGs from the throwaway CDP harness. The Supabase backend is not configured in this sandbox, so the flows use mocked APIs and a fixture wallet. The audit is ../audit/final-audit.jsonl.
