Onboarding UI - current review

Welcome, Create your vault, Privacy, Name, Balance error and the Nimiq Pay gate at 360, 390 and 430. Corrections go in NOTES.md, under the screen they belong to and naming the width where possible.

Before - the old /setup wizard

/setup - one page, five labelled dots, square panels, mint CTA

1. Welcome

/welcome - Brand lockup, character art, headline, coral CTA, "Built for Nimiq Pay".

2. Create your vault - intro

/connect - Step 1 of 3, numbered explainer, can/cannot cards, coral CTA.

3. Create your vault - account picker

/connect - Approved wallet account; the identity is never displayed after this step.

4. Create your vault - 24 words

/connect - The existing ceremony, unchanged: 24 words, acknowledgement, Continue.

5. Create your vault - quiz

/connect - Four words from the written copy; the fields are the existing ceremony.

6. Create your vault - lock

/connect - Passphrase encrypts the phrase at rest; 8 characters minimum.

7. Create your vault - ready

/connect - Returning from Privacy shows the locked state and Continue.

8. Privacy notice

/privacy - Step 2 of 3: short version, four cards, consent line and coral Continue.

9. Name your character

/name - Step 3 of 3: character preview, empty field and disabled CTA.

10. Name - 16 characters

/name - Counter at 16/16; the CTA is enabled.

11. Name - error

/name - A server rejection renders the coral banner below the field.

12. Balance error

/balance-error - Only for 503 rpc_unreachable; Try again leaves a one-shot flag for Home.

13. Gate - Preparing

/welcome?gate=preparing - Brand mark and one line; no spinner, no action.

14. Gate - Open inside Nimiq Pay

/welcome?gate=outside - Phone panel and explanation; no CTA.

15. Gate - Update Nimiq Pay

/welcome?gate=update - Update arrow, site link and the retry that re-runs the check.

Renders are 2x device-scale PNGs from the throwaway CDP harness. The Supabase backend is not configured in this sandbox, so the flows use mocked APIs and a fixture wallet. The audit is ../audit/final-audit.jsonl.
